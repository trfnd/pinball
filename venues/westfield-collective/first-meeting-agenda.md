# Westfield Collective: first meeting agenda

_Contact: Angie (GM). Status: open to discussing, and nothing more. Meeting not yet scheduled._

**Goal:** learn, don't close. Leave with facts and a clear next step, such as a site walkthrough with measurements.

## What to learn

- [ ] **Her goal.** Is pinball an amenity to keep guests longer, or an income stream? Any past experience with games or arcade vendors?
- [ ] **Who decides.** Angie, an owner or a landlord? Does a lease limit use of shared space?
- [ ] **Space.** Candidate spots, floor space and room to play, delivery access (doors, steps), power outlets and circuits, Wi-Fi or cell signal, spill and sun exposure.
- [ ] **Audience.** Busy days and hours, family versus adult mix, rules and hours for minors. Can we see hourly transaction counts?
- [ ] **Staff role.** Out-of-order signs, power-cycling machines, refunds, how to contact the owner.
- [ ] **Terms.** Her expectations on the split, reporting, electricity, insurance (additional insured?) and trial length. Ask before proposing 70/30.
- [ ] **Events.** Would tournaments or family nights be welcome, and how often?
- [ ] **Timing.** Is a December install realistic, or is it a bad month?
- [ ] **Deal-breakers.** What would make this a no?

## Do not

- Promise specific machines, an opening date or an uptime level.
- Suggest that any other venue has agreed to host machines.
