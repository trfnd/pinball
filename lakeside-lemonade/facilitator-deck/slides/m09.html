Anthropic: Building effective agents · Multi-agent research system · Economic Index

This is the central idea of Part 2. A better model alone doesn't make work reliable; the process around it does. Anthropic says so twice. In "Building effective agents": "whereas automated testing helps verify functionality, human review remains crucial for ensuring solutions align with broader system requirements." In its research-system write-up, human testers caught a bias the automated judges missed: "Even in a world of automated evaluations, manual testing remains essential." This will sound familiar: QC plans, monitoring plans and data review plans are all reliability designed in advance. What an independent check looks for: the rubric Anthropic gave its AI judge for research outputs — factual accuracy (do claims match sources?), citation accuracy (do the cited sources say that?), completeness, and source quality (primary over weaker secondary sources). Add independence: the checker sees the work and the sources, not the writer's reasoning, so it can't be talked into agreeing. Pair it with "verify with citations": retract any claim without a supporting quote. Checking is part of the real cost: Anthropic's January 2026 Economic Index found that adjusting productivity estimates for how reliably tasks succeed roughly halves the expected gain, from 1.8 to about 1.0 percentage points a year. Discussion: groups, 5 minutes, then hear two. Ground it in Diligence: the AI Fluency course defines Deployment Diligence as "Taking responsibility for verifying and vouching for the outputs you use or share." Anthropic's Usage Policy lists healthcare as a high-risk use and, for uses it covers, requires that "a qualified professional in that field must review the content or decision prior to dissemination or finalization." Caveat: that's framed around consumer-facing uses — present it as a stated principle, not a legal ruling, and check with QA and regulatory colleagues. Follow-up: "Would you hand it to someone else if you weren't watching every run?"
