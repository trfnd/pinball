Closing

Four questions to take back to your desk.

Delegation

Should AI be part of this task at all, and which part?

Description

Would a brilliant new colleague understand my brief?

Discernment

What did I check, and against what, before trusting it?

Diligence

Whose name is on it, and who knows AI was involved?

Bring the day back to the four Ds. Take three or four aloud. The AI Fluency course ends with "a reminder that AI systems are powerful but not magical solutions; they're only as useful and safe as we enable them to be through our thoughtful engagement." That sums up the day: the lemonade was practice, and the habits are what you take with you. Encourage: everyone in the room did real work today — briefing, checking, delegating, designing a check, naming an owner. Those habits don't go out of date when the tools change.
