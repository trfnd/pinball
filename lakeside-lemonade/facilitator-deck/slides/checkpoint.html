Lakeside Lemonade · Debrief

Dell'Acqua et al., Organization Science 2026 · AI Fluency course

Your checkpoint build was a small test of a big idea. Dell'Acqua and colleagues gave 758 BCG consultants 18 realistic tasks, with or without GPT-4. The study ran in 2023 and was published in Organization Science in 2026. Inside the frontier, AI users completed 12.2% more tasks, 25.1% faster, with significantly better quality. On a task designed to sit just outside it, they were 19 percentage points less likely to be correct. Co-author Ethan Mollick's plain version: about 84% got that task right without AI, and 60 to 70% with it. Below-average performers gained the most inside the frontier (+43% vs +17% in the 2023 working paper). Caveats: one firm, consulting tasks, a 2023 model. What carries over: the frontier is invisible and uneven, so you map it yourself, task by task. Activity: the AI Fluency course splits Delegation into problem awareness (understand the goal first), platform awareness (know what this tool can do, and whether it's approved for this kind of document) and task delegation (who does which part). Its key line: "The goal isn't to automate everything, but to create the most effective human-AI partnership for any given task or goal." Give 4 minutes to sketch three columns for one real document. Example map (ours, not Anthropic's): human only — rationale, endpoint choice, eligibility decisions, safety rules, sign-off; human plus AI — background drafted from literature you supply, plain-language first drafts; AI does it, you check — reformatting tables, standardizing terminology, cross-reference checklists. Then ask: "What would you move? What would never move?" Transition: in Part 2, this map becomes a permission list.
