Anthropic: Multi-agent research system · AI Fluency (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) · Usage Policy

Anthropic's engineers, on taking their research agent from prototype to production: "When building AI agents, the last mile often becomes most of the journey." Their advice is to start simple: "you should consider adding complexity only when it demonstrably improves outcomes," which "might mean not building agentic systems at all." And test early with small samples: "We started with a set of about 20 queries representing real usage patterns." For a study team, 20 past documents with known issues is a realistic first test. The five-part brief maps onto the morning's framework. Problem is Delegation's problem awareness. Data covers platform awareness and Creation Diligence ("Being thoughtful about which AI systems you use and how you interact with them"). The human checkpoint is where Discernment turns into a decision. The owner carries Deployment Diligence. Who loses is Transparency Diligence: "Being honest about AI's role in your work with everyone who needs to know." Anthropic's AI Fluency Index notes that 13 of its 24 fluency behaviors happen outside the chat window, calling them "arguably some of the most consequential dimensions of AI fluency." Disclosure: the AI Fluency course published its own AI Diligence Statement, a useful model: "All AI-generated and co-created content underwent thorough validation, editing, and curation by the human authors... the human authors maintain responsibility for the content." Anthropic's Usage Policy: "end-users should be aware when AI has been involved in producing outputs." Check your journal, sponsor, institution and ethics committee rules, which may be stricter. Discussion: pairs, 5 minutes. "Who loses" is where briefs are least honest, so make it social — let the neighbor play that person. Then shrink the plan: a pilot on known-answer documents (Module 08's test) is cheaper and safer than a full launch. Close the loop with the opening flip chart: did anyone's "document I'd love to spend less time on" make it into their brief?
