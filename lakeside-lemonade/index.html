Lakeside Lemonade· GenAI Strategy & Execution

From a lemonade stand to GenAI strategy

Ten short modules. One small business. Everything you'd actually do with AI.

A hands-on course · adapted from the HBS Executive Education original

You'll plan a real lemonade stand in the Lakeside neighborhood — who it's for, where it stands, what it charges, how it sounds, and how it launches — and in doing it you'll learn the moves that matter with AI: prompting, local expertise, clarifying questions, skills, agents, and agent swarms. Each module ends with a short reflection; at the end you hand in one report, in your own words.

  4. Brand — a Skill writes your brand book

  5. Finale — your deployment brief + report
  6. Bonus — bring agents into your world
