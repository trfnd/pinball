Karen Brennan's Vibe Coding course, Harvard Graduate School of Education.

You just took a project from nothing to a shareable website, with AI, in five short steps. That pattern wasn't about lemonade. It's about you being able to make a real thing out of an idea now — so make one. Not for a grade. Because you can.

1Choose something small and true

Pick one of the four prompts on the left. The best first builds are embarrassingly specific — a tool only your family needs, a page that answers one question you keep re-explaining, a toy that makes one person laugh. Specific beats impressive.

2Give Claude what only you know

The same lesson from Module 02 applies: the thing that makes it yours is the context you bring. Tell Claude who it's for, what it needs to do, and the details that live only in your head. Then build, look, correct, build again.

I want to build a small [web page / tool / toy] that [does one specific thing] for [who it's for]. Here's what you need to know that you can't guess: [the details only you have]. Build a first version I can look at, then we'll fix it together.

When you've got something — even half-working — come back and write down what happened. The questions on the right are the whole point of the checkpoint: they're where the doing turns into something you'll remember.
