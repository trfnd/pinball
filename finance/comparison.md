## What to collect from every venue

These are the venue inputs, in rough order of effect on the result:

1. **Play volume**: hourly traffic, dwell time, age mix and hours for minors. Sets `games_per_machine_week`.
2. **Terms**: split, any rent or monthly minimum, who pays electricity. Any rent or minimum raises break-even play for every lineup.
3. **Space**: how many machines fit (`machine_slots`), power, delivery access (`moving_per_machine`).
4. **Insurance**: requirements such as additional-insured status (`insurance_per_year`).
5. **Local rules**: amusement-device licensing (`license_per_machine_year`).
6. **Distance**: round trip from home (`round_trip_miles`).
7. **Price mix**: a family crowd buying 3 for $2 pulls `price_per_game` toward $0.67.

## Not modeled

- Novelty spikes, seasonality and events. Play is flat month to month.
- Guest games are modeled as one swap at month 4. Repeated short runs would add a move and a few team hours per swap.
- Income tax, depreciation deductions, and the cost of capital tied up in machines.
- Theft, vandalism or a major failure beyond the maintenance allowance.
- Keeping purchased machines at exit instead of selling them (collection value, not cash).
- Team time (Ibrahim and Amy) is counted in hours, not split between them, and not given a dollar value.
