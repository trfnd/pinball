_Generated by `finance/model.py`. Do not edit by hand; change the inputs in the script and rerun._

**Every number here is an assumption or a placeholder.** No venue terms, purchase quotes, resale listings or play counts exist yet. Use this to see which inputs matter and what to find out first, not as a forecast.

## How to read this

- **Scenarios** (weak / middle / strong) vary play volume, price mix, maintenance, resale values and wear together. Deal terms and fixed costs are the same in all three.
- **Lineups** cover the open question of what is already owned. Transformers and Dune are confirmed owned. Whether Star Wars and Pokémon are owned is **open**. `+R` adds the proposed Feb/Mar 2027 replacement of Transformers and Dune with two purchased machines.
- **Cash result** = −new cash + operating cash + net resale − exit costs. This is what the bank account shows after exit.
- **Incl. wear** also subtracts value lost on owned collection machines from public play. That isn't cash, but it is a real cost.
- ⚠ marks a result worse than the −$5,000 working loss tolerance.

## Not modeled

- Rent, minimum payments or revenue guarantees to the venue. Any of these would make every scenario worse.
- Novelty spikes, seasonality and events. Play is flat month to month.
- Income tax, depreciation deductions, and the cost of capital tied up in machines.
- Theft, vandalism or a major failure beyond the maintenance allowance.
- Keeping purchased machines at exit instead of selling them (that turns resale into collection value, not cash).
- Owner time is counted in hours and not given a dollar value.
