How we operate

You bring the place. We bring the play.

  • Machines

    We supply the games, deliver them and set them up.

  • Upkeep

    Maintenance, repairs, player support and refunds are on us.

  • Payments

    Guests pay at the machine by cash, card or phone, separate from your checkout.

  • Players

    We list your venue on Pinball Map and can host beginner nights or high‑score challenges.

Your partA good spot, power and access for service visits. We pick the spot together and agree each side’s responsibilities up front.

Business model

We fund it. You get a share.

  1. Guests play

    Guests pay per game at the machine.

  2. We share

    Play revenue is split on terms we agree in writing before anything is installed.

  3. We review

    After the first operating period, we look at play, reliability and guest feedback together.
