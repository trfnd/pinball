Who we are

Hi, I’m Ibrahim.

I started Paw Tap Pinball, a small pinball operator in central Indiana. I’m a physician and a pinball collector, and I maintain and repair my own machines.

Paw Tap is how I share the fun: friends around a game, a parent teaching the flippers, one more try at a high score.

How we operate

You bring the place. We bring the play.

  • Machines

    We supply the games, deliver them and set them up.

  • Upkeep

    Maintenance, repairs, player support and refunds are on us.

  • Payments

    Guests pay at the machine by cash, card or phone, separate from your checkout.

  • Players

    We list your venue on Pinball Map and can host beginner nights or high‑score challenges.

Your partA good spot, power and access for service visits. We choose the location together and put each side’s responsibilities in writing.

Business model

We fund it. You share the receipts.

  1. We invest

    We provide the machines and pay for their upkeep. You buy nothing.

  2. We share

    Play revenue is split on terms we agree in writing before anything is installed.

  3. We review

    After the first operating period, we look at play, reliability and guest feedback together.
