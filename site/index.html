How we operate

You provide the place. We look after the play.

Paw Tap Pinball funds, installs and runs the games. Routine play is self-service, so your staff don't run anything.

  • Our part

    The machines

    We supply the games, deliver them and set them up.

  • Our part

    The upkeep

    Maintenance, repairs, player support and refunds.

  • For guests

    Pay at the game

    Cash, card or phone, separate from your checkout.

  • For players

    Easy to find

    Your venue goes on Pinball Map. Beginner nights and high-score challenges if you want them.

Your partA good spot, a power outlet and access for service visits. We pick the spot together.

Business model

Operator-funded. Revenue shared.

The venue buys no machines. Both sides share in play receipts.

  1. We invest

    Paw Tap Pinball provides the machines and pays for their upkeep.

  2. Guests play

    Every game is paid for at the machine.

  3. We share

    Receipts are split on terms we agree together before anything is installed.

  4. We review

    After the first operating period, we look at play, reliability and guest feedback together.

No machines to buy. No games to run.
